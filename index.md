<section class="home-section">
  <div class="section-title-wrap">
    <h2 class="section-title">Core Research Pillars</h2>
    <div class="section-accent-bar"></div>
  </div>
  <p class="page-lead">
    Bridging experimental communication science, cognitive presence theory, and computational design across four foundational thrusts.
  </p>

  <div class="pillars-grid">
    <div class="pillar-card">
      <div class="pillar-card__icon">
        <i class="fa-solid fa-brain"></i>
      </div>
      <h3 class="pillar-card__title">1. Generative AI &amp; Human-Agent Dynamics</h3>
      <p class="pillar-card__desc">
        Examining psychological trust calibration, algorithmic appreciation, conversational ergonomics, and cognitive mental models when individuals interact with generative language models, co-pilot architectures, and autonomous decision systems.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="pillar-card">
      <div class="pillar-card__icon">
        <i class="fa-solid fa-vr-cardboard"></i>
      </div>
      <h3 class="pillar-card__title">2. Cognitive Presence &amp; Immersive XR</h3>
      <p class="pillar-card__desc">
        Advancing theoretical and empirical formulations of spatial presence, social presence, and self-presence (including the Proteus effect) across head-mounted displays, extended reality (XR), and synthetic spatial environments.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="pillar-card">
      <div class="pillar-card__icon">
        <i class="fa-solid fa-robot"></i>
      </div>
      <h3 class="pillar-card__title">3. Social Robotics &amp; Embodied Interaction</h3>
      <p class="pillar-card__desc">
        Investigating anthropomorphism, social attribution, and nonverbal signaling in embodied physical robots, smart vehicular cockpits, speech user interfaces (SUI), and multimodal agents.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="pillar-card">
      <div class="pillar-card__icon">
        <i class="fa-solid fa-heart-pulse"></i>
      </div>
      <h3 class="pillar-card__title">4. UX Ergonomics &amp; Digital Well-Being</h3>
      <p class="pillar-card__desc">
        Translating empirical behavioral science into human-centered design frameworks. Investigating the psychological effects of AI in mental health communication, information verification, and digital habituation.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
