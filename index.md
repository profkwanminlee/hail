  <h2>Investigating the Frontiers of Human-AI Interaction</h2>
  <p class="lead-text">
    The Human-AI Interaction Lab (HAIL) and Interaction Lab at Nanyang Technological University explore to answer one central question: <strong>what happens in the human mind, behaviour and society when we interact with AI (or AI agents) that increasingly look, act, and think like us?</strong>
  </p>
  <p>
    Drawing on cognitive neuroscience, psychology, artificial intelligence, and communication, we study how people perceive, trust, and interact with AI agents, robots, avatars, and autonomous technologies in physical and virtual environments.
  </p>
  <p>
    Operating at the intersection of the Wee Kim Wee School of Communication and Information (WKWSCI) and the Asian Communication Research Centre (ACRC), the lab combines empirical human experiments, neurophysiological measurement, and computational methods to understand social presence, user experience, and the psychological impact of intelligent machines.
  </p>

  <h2>Core Research Thrusts</h2>
  <p>
    Our interdisciplinary investigations span five integrated research pillars:
  </p>
  <ul class="clean-bullet-list">
    <li><strong>Human-AI Interaction:</strong> Communication dynamics, trust, and user experience with conversational agents, embodied robots, and autonomous systems.</li>
    <li><strong>Perception &amp; Agent Design:</strong> Visual perception of human, robotic, avatar, and deepfake faces, and social presence across virtual and physical spaces.</li>
    <li><strong>Trust &amp; Human-Technology Dynamics:</strong> Psychological and neural mechanisms of trust formation in autonomous mobility, fintech, and healthcare.</li>
    <li><strong>AI Ethics, Philosophy &amp; Policy:</strong> Ethical agent design, philosophical implications of synthetic minds, and evidence for AI governance.</li>
    <li><strong>AI-Enhanced Research &amp; Media Integrity:</strong> Synthetic panels, artificial societies, and deep learning for detecting synthetic media and misinformation.</li>
  </ul>
